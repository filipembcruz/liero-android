## English (default)
**Title (30):** Tunnel Clash
**Short description (80):** Dig, swing and blast! Fast 2D tunnel deathmatch against the CPU. No ads.
**Full description:**
Tunnel Clash is a fast, chaotic 2D deathmatch where the whole map is destructible. Dig tunnels, swing on your ninja rope and pick 5 weapons out of 40 — bazookas, mines, lasers, nukes and the legendary banana bomb — to take down the CPU.

• Classic, faithful gameplay inspired by the legendary PC game Liero
• 40 weapons with exact original physics
• Ninja rope, tunnelling and fully destructible terrain
• CPU opponent with 3 difficulty levels
• 4 drawn looks (Wigglers, Moles, Soldiers, Robots) plus the original pixel style
• 10 map themes: Earth, Desert, Snow, Moon, Mars, Chocolate and more
• Touch controls designed for phones, fully configurable — or play with a Bluetooth gamepad
• Achievements and statistics
• No ads, no pay-to-win, no data collection

Tunnel Clash is made by a single independent developer. If you enjoy it, you can support development with an optional tip inside the game — it unlocks nothing but a heart next to your name, and helps bring multiplayer and new maps.
